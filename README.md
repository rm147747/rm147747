# Brandão Lab

**Clinical AI and computational oncology at the University of Florida College of Medicine, Jacksonville.**

Led by Raphael Brandão Moreira, MD, Associate Professor of Medicine, Division of Hematology and Oncology. Medical oncologist in genitourinary cancers and melanoma. Dana-Farber trained. The lab builds and evaluates AI systems for oncology decisions, and builds the teaching tools that come out of that work.

## Research lines

**1. Clinical AI and multi-agent systems.** Building decision-support systems for oncology and measuring whether they help: how large language models reason about oncology decisions, alone and when they debate each other, and how that should be evaluated. Deployed platforms, pre-registered protocols, frozen synthetic cases, blinded human scoring, open evaluation harnesses.

**2. AI-built medical education.** Teaching material for fellows, residents and students produced with agent pipelines and reviewed by a clinician: bedside notes from rounds, tumor-specific updates, interactive courses that teach a method through one of our own studies.

**3. Computational oncology and variant interpretation.** Pathogenicity prediction for variants of uncertain significance in homologous recombination repair genes, chromosome 3p biology in kidney cancer, and evolutionary models for adaptive therapy.

**4. Early detection.** Corresponding author of OncoSeek, an AI multi-cancer early detection test validated in 15,122 participants across seven centers in three countries (npj Precision Oncology, 2025).

**5. Agents for drug development and clinical trials.** Agentic systems for the work that sits between a drug and a patient: eligibility screening of real clinical notes against recruiting trials, structuring of protocol criteria, pre-screening and accrual support at the site level, and evaluation of how these agents fail before they are trusted. First component built: OncoMatch. Next: site-level pre-screening pilots at UF Health Jacksonville and evaluation protocols for trial-facing agents, in the same pre-registered, blinded design used in onc-bench.

## Projects

| Project | What it is | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OncoIA | Oncology decision-support platform launched in February 2025: hybrid retrieval (dense embeddings plus BM25 with reciprocal rank fusion) over 368 NCCN-anchored clinical scenarios across 23 tumor types, and a five-agent virtual tumor board | Closed research platform, access by invitation for experiments |
| OncoMatch | Clinical trial matching module inside OncoIA: AI eligibility screening of a free-text clinical note against 13,901 recruiting trials from ClinicalTrials.gov, 16 tumor types, 80 or more biomarkers, country filter, about 20 seconds per screen | Closed research platform, access by invitation for experiments |
| onc-bench | Pre-registered harness that tests frontier language models against GU oncology cases: four vendor adapters, debate arms, resumable call ledger, blinded scoring, statistical analysis plan written before any data | Harness complete, case authoring under way |
| OncoBoardBench | Platform for longitudinal evaluation of AI-supported oncology decisions on frozen synthetic cases with blinded expert review | Study phase |
| AlphaHRD | Four pathogenicity predictors benchmarked against survival for HRR missense variants across 31 TCGA cancer types | Manuscript under review |
| Computational Tumor Board | Four-layer evolutionary modeling framework for adaptive cancer therapy | Prototype |
| [OncoTricks](https://www.oncotricks.com) | Short, sourced teaching notes from inpatient rounds, for fellows, residents and students | Live, updated after rounds |
| Idea Tournament | Multi-agent system that proposes, screens, verifies and ranks research ideas for trainees | Prototype |

Repositories are opened as each study is released. Everything here uses synthetic or public data only; no patient data is stored in this account.
